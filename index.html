Wonjune Kim

Ph.D. student at KAIST

I am a Ph.D. student in AI Computing at KAIST, where I work in the Computer Vision and Learning Lab (KCVL) advised by Prof. Tae‑Kyun Kim. My research interests are in robot learning, with a focus on dexterous manipulation, world models, and hand–object interaction.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Robotics from KAIST under Prof. Dongsuk Kum (AXELAB), where I worked on label-efficient 3D perception for autonomous driving, such as active learning for 3D occupancy prediction and camera–radar fusion. I also spent time at ETRI, working on perception for unstructured driving environments and LiDAR-based vegetation measurement. I completed my B.S. in Automobile and IT Convergence at Kookmin University.
